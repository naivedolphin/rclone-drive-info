Privacy Policy

Personal rclone Drive is a personal desktop configuration of rclone, operated by its owner to access their Google Drive from their own computer.

Effective date and last updated: October 23, 2026

本配置仅供所有者个人使用。网盘文件由本机 rclone 与 Google Drive 直接交换；授权凭证、备份和文件缓存保存在所有者电脑上。本文档网站不接收网盘文件或授权令牌。

1. Information accessed

When authorized by the owner, rclone can access Google Drive file and folder metadata, file contents, and storage information needed to display and operate the mounted drive. It uses OAuth access and refresh tokens to authenticate requests.

The requested Google Drive scope permits viewing, modifying, creating, and deleting files. The owner chooses the Google account during authorization and controls use of the mounted drive.

2. How information is used

Google user data is used only to provide the owner's requested file operations: listing, reading, downloading, uploading, editing, renaming, moving, and deleting files, and displaying available storage. Access may also be exercised by software on the owner's computer that the owner permits to use the mounted drive.

Google Drive data is not used by this personal application for advertising, profiling, sale, or training AI or machine-learning models.

3. Storage and security

File operations occur between the owner's local rclone process and Google Drive. OAuth credentials, tokens, configuration backups, logs, and cached file contents may be stored locally on the owner's computer. Cached files and backups can remain after a program exits.

The owner is responsible for restricting access to the computer and its configuration, backup, log, and cache locations. Anyone who can access usable credentials or the mounted drive may be able to access Drive data. Google API communication uses HTTPS.

4. Sharing and transfers

This personal configuration has no separate application server receiving the owner's Google Drive data. It does not sell Google user data or transfer it to advertising or data-broker services. Google processes API requests under its own policies. The owner can choose to copy or share files using their computer or Google Drive.

Personal rclone Drive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

Google Drive files remain in the owner's Google account until managed or deleted by the owner. Local downloaded files, caches, configuration backups, and logs are retained until removed by the owner or applicable cache settings.

To stop this application, the owner can unmount the drive, stop rclone, and revoke the application's access. Revoking authorization does not automatically erase downloaded files, caches, or backups; these must be removed separately if desired. Deleting the OAuth client also does not delete the owner's Drive files.

6. Revoking access

The owner can review and revoke this application's access at any time through Google Account connections. New Google Drive access will then require authorization again.

7. This documentation website

These pages contain static application and policy information. No forms, Google login, analytics scripts, or Drive API integrations are included in their source. No Google OAuth tokens, client secrets, or Drive files are published here.

The website hosting provider may process ordinary web-request and security logs under its own policies. Visiting these pages does not authorize access to Google Drive.

8. Contact and changes

Contact the application owner through the support email displayed on Google's authorization screen. Changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
