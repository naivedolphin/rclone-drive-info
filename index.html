Personal desktop application

Your Drive, on your own computer.

Personal rclone Drive is the owner's personal configuration of the open-source rclone desktop tool. It connects the owner's Google Drive to a drive letter on their Windows computer.

这是个人使用的 rclone 网盘挂载配置，用于把 Google Drive 挂载为 Windows 本地盘符。

What the application does

  • Lists folders and files in the Google Drive account chosen during authorization.
  • Downloads and opens files when requested by the owner or software running on the owner's computer.
  • Uploads, edits, renames, moves, and deletes files when instructed through the mounted drive or rclone commands.

Why Google Drive access is requested

The application requests the Google Drive permission to view, edit, create, and delete files, so that the mounted drive can support ordinary file operations. Google authorization is performed through the owner's Google account.

Personal use. This OAuth application is operated by its owner for their own desktop use. These public documentation pages do not provide a Google sign-in or request access to visitors' Drive accounts.

How data is handled

rclone runs on the owner's computer and communicates with Google Drive for file operations. OAuth credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, configuration backups, and optional file caches are stored on the owner's computer. The documentation website has no application backend that stores Google Drive files or credentials.

Read the Privacy Policy for details about permissions, local storage, retention, and revoking access.

Terms of personal use

This configuration is intended for the owner's authorized access to their own Google Drive. The owner controls file operations, backups, and local computer access. Google services remain subject to Google's terms and service limits. rclone is distributed under its open-source license.

Availability depends on the local computer, network, Google APIs, and valid authorization. The owner is responsible for maintaining suitable backups of important data.

Contact

For questions about this personal application, contact its owner using the support email shown on the Google authorization screen.
